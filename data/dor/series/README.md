# City of Spokane taxable retail sales, annual, 2014 to 2025. Pulled 2026-11-16.

Source: WA Dept. of Revenue, Taxable Retail Sales query tool (Local).
Location 3210 Spokane (City), tax type Sales, all NAICS, Annual periods.
https://apps.dor.wa.gov/ResearchStats/Content/TaxableRetailSalesLocal/Report.aspx

These are DOR's own Annual rows, not sums of the quarters. The two can
differ by a rounding-scale amount (under 0.05%) because of annual filers
and amended returns. One series, one convention.

Caveat: ESSB 5814 extended retail sales tax to several services effective
October 1, 2025, so 2025 includes one quarter on a broader tax base and
every later year compares against it. The 2025 growth figure (+0.1%) is,
if anything, slightly flattered by the change.
https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/retail-sales-tax/services-newly-subject-retail-sales-tax
