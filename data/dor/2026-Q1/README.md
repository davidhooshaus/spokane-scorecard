# WA Dept. of Revenue taxable retail sales, pulled 2026-11-16

City series: DOR Taxable Retail Sales query tool (Local), location 3210
Spokane (City), tax type Sales, All NAICS.
https://apps.dor.wa.gov/ResearchStats/Content/TaxableRetailSalesLocal/Report.aspx

County and state Q1 comparison: DOR Quarterly Business Review, Table 3a,
County Taxable Retail Sales Comparing 1st Quarter 2025 to 1st Quarter 2026.
https://apps.dor.wa.gov/ResearchStats/Content/QuarterlyBusinessReview/Results3.aspx?Period=2026Q1&Type=naics&Format=HTML

Caveat that must travel with any year-over-year comparison that crosses
October 1, 2025: ESSB 5814 extended retail sales tax to several services
(IT services, custom software, custom website development, advertising,
security and investigation, temporary staffing, live presentations) effective
that date, so growth rates from Q4 2025 on compare a broader tax base against
a narrower one and overstate real spending growth. City, county, and state
all share the same base, so comparisons between them remain clean.
https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/retail-sales-tax/services-newly-subject-retail-sales-tax
