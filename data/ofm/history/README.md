# WA OFM April 1 population, Spokane County and state, 2010 to 2026. Pulled 2026-11-16.

spokane_state_april1_2010_2026.csv merges two official OFM series:
- 2010 to 2019: intercensal estimates (revised after the 2020 census),
  ofm_april1_intercensal_estimates_2010_2020.xlsx
- 2020: the 2020 census count. 2021 to 2026: postcensal estimates,
  ofm_april1_population_final.xlsx
Both from https://ofm.wa.gov/data-research/population-demographics/estimates/
(April 1 official estimates page and its archive page).

The series column labels which regime each year comes from. OFM itself
treats these as one continuous April 1 series.
