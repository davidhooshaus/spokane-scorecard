# WA OFM April 1, 2026 official population estimates, pulled 2026-11-16

Source: Office of Financial Management, April 1 official population
estimates, published June 29, 2026. Press release tables 1 and 2.
https://ofm.wa.gov/data-research/population-demographics/estimates/april-1-official/

Spokane County grew 0.81% from April 2025 to April 2026 (4,600 people),
against 0.75% for the state. Net migration was 4,081 of the county's
4,600-person gain, about nine in ten. County growth slowed from 1.18%
the year before (559,400 to 566,000).
