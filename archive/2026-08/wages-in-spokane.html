The minimum wage in Spokane, and what jobs really pay

The current rate. Why it changes every January. What eight local industries pay on average. Every figure links to a public source. The page refreshes each quarter with the scorecard.

The minimum wage in Spokane

The minimum wage in Spokane is $17.13 an hour in 2026. Spokane has no city minimum wage, so Washington State’s rate applies.

  • The state rate is set by the Washington Dept. of Labor & Industries. It adjusts for inflation every year. The new rate is announced on September 30. It takes effect January 1.
  • Tips do not count against it. Washington requires the full minimum wage on top of tips and service charges. L&I.
  • Eight Washington cities and counties set higher local rates. Bellingham is lowest ($19.13) and Tukwila is highest ($21.65). All eight are in western Washington. No eastern Washington city has one, Spokane included. L&I local minimum wage rates.
  • Washington’s minimum is among the highest of any state. It raises the floor under every job here. The scorecard notes it pushed Spokane’s hourly pay close to the U.S. average.

What the average job pays here

  • The average wage across the Spokane metro is $32.96 an hour (May 2025; the metro is Spokane County plus Stevens County). The state minimum is about half of that. BLS Occupational Employment and Wages.
  • Average pay per worker in Spokane County was $68,949 in 2025. It has climbed for a decade. BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages.

What it means

The floor is high here. The middle is average. A minimum-wage job in Spokane pays more than one in most states. The harder finding is about the middle. Income per person is 97% of the U.S. figure and 80% of Washington’s. And the sectors hiring fastest pay near the middle, not the top. The full picture is on the scorecard and in plain terms on Is Spokane a good place to live.

Where these numbers come from

The minimum wage comes from the Washington Dept. of Labor & Industries. Hourly averages come from the BLS Occupational Employment and Wages release for the Spokane metro. Annual pay comes from the BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages for Spokane County. Two industry figures come from Lightcast via the Spokane Workforce Council. Those two are labeled as privately sourced. Every figure carries its geography and date.

This page was last updated in September 2026. The minimum wage changes every January 1, and this page is rechecked each quarter. If a number here is wrong, say so, and it gets fixed in public with a note in the change log.
