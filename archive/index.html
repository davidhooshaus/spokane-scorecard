Past editions

Every quarterly edition, frozen exactly as it was published. Old figures are never revised in place and old grades are never quietly upgraded. If something here turns out to be wrong, the correction goes in a new entry in the change log, and the original stays visible.

Why this page exists

A scorecard that can edit its own past is not a scorecard. The whole claim this project makes is that the numbers were what they were on the day they were published, and that anyone can check whether a grade moved because the data moved or because the argument got inconvenient.

So each quarter, before any figure is touched, the current edition is copied here unchanged. The live page moves forward. This page does not.
