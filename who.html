Who made this

A page that grades a region should say who is doing the grading. Anonymous civic advocacy invites the question “who is really behind this,” and that question is more damaging than any disclosure would be.

How to correct it

Corrections are the point, not an inconvenience. If a figure is wrong, a source is stale, or a grade does not follow the published rubric, send it and it gets fixed.

  • Corrections get posted, not quietly patched. Every change appears as a new entry in the change log, with what changed and why.
  • Past editions are never edited. Each quarter’s edition is frozen in past editions before any figure is touched. If an archived edition contains an error, the correction goes in the live change log and the original stays visible.
  • Grades change only when the data changes, never because of a news cycle or a phone call.
  • Better data wins. If an institution publishes something that contradicts a number here, this page links it and updates.
